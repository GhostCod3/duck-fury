KEEP YOUR FEATHERS.

Play as Rook against the Dustjaw bot. One bullet ends a round. The first duck to win five rounds takes the match.

MOVE
A / D or ← / →. Touch: movement pad.
JUMP
Space / W / ↑ / K. Press again for a double jump.
FIRE
Hold J / Z or FIRE. Shoot in the direction you face.
EQUIP
E or EQUIP near a weapon to pick it up or swap.
DROP
S / ↓, or drag down on the pad, to drop through a raised platform.
PAUSE
Esc / P or the pause button. Leaving the tab pauses play.

Weapons have limited ammo. Swap an empty gun. Rockets can kill their owner. Each new round resets weapons and changes the arena. A 45-second stalemate is a draw.

Standard gamepads / DualShock labels: left stick or D-pad move · Cross jump/double jump · Square fire · Circle equip/swap · Options pause/resume. Release held controls after reconnecting or resuming. Physical DualShock 4 + OnePlus 13 / Chrome testing is pending.
